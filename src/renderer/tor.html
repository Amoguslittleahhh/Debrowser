Nothing from this window is written to disk: no history, no cookies kept, no thumbnails, no crash dumps. Everything connects through Tor or not at all – if Tor stops, pages stop loading rather than connecting directly.

When your safety depends on it, use Tor Browser or Tails. Websites can still tell this browser apart more easily than Tor Browser, and a program running as you on this computer can read what this window holds.

This is the ordinary browser. Private windows open with Ctrl+Shift+N.
